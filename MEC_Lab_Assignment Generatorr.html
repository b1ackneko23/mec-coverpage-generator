Enter each serial number and title once. These entries populate the index and the individual cover pages. Pages are generated in serial-number order.

Page order: main cover → index page(s) → individual covers in serial-number order. Index pagination: up to 9 entries on one page; when there are more than 9, page 1 holds 8 and later pages hold up to 9. The index signature appears only on its last page. Use your browser's print dialog and select A4 paper, scale 100%, margins none/default as indicated.
